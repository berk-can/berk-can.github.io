# Winggy Plane - Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 3 November 2026

Winggy Plane ("the game") is made by Can Make Game, an independent developer based in Türkiye ("I", "me"). This policy
explains what information the game handles on Android and iOS, phones and tablets. If you have a question, write to
berkcanmakegame@gmail.com.

## The short version

- There are no accounts and no login. I do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts or location.
- Your progress (coins, upgrades, fighters, settings) is saved **only on your device**. I never receive it.
- The game shows ads from **Google AdMob**. Google may collect some device and usage data to show ads, measure them and
  prevent fraud - see "Advertising" below.
- Players under 13 get child-directed, non-personalised ads only, with no tracking.
- I do not sell or share your personal data, and I do not run any analytics or crash reporting of my own.

## 1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress and preferences on your device: coins, upgrades, the fighters you own and the one you selected,
best score, settings (language, dark mode, vibration, left-handed layout, motion-control sensitivity), which tutorial hints you
have seen, the birth year you entered on the age screen (or only the result: under or over 13), and how many bonus-gold
videos you have watched today. This is stored in the app's private storage (some of it encrypted) and is never sent to me or
to anyone else.

**Deleting it:** uninstall the game, or clear its data in your device settings. That removes everything the game stored.
Because I never receive this data, there is nothing else for me to delete.

## 2. Age screen

The first time you start the game, a neutral screen asks for your birth year. It is used only on your device to decide how
ads are handled (section 3). The answer is not sent to me, to Google or to anyone else - only the resulting setting
("child-directed" or not) affects the ad requests.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK), provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited:
a banner at the top of the screen, a full-screen ad after every fifth FLY AGAIN, and optional reward videos (TRY AGAIN +
SHIELD and +GOLD) that you choose to watch.

To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process, for example:
- device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID, the App Set ID or, on iOS, the IDFA (only if you allow tracking) and
  the IDFV;
- your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (country / city level);
- information about how you interact with ads (views, taps, video completion);
- diagnostic and performance information about the SDK and your device (device model, operating system, app version).

Google uses this to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see them, and detect fraud and abuse. How Google uses
information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites . Google's privacy
policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy .

**Players 13 and over**
- **EEA, UK and Switzerland:** before any ad is requested, Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) asks whether you
  agree to personalised ads and related data uses. You can accept, reject or choose options. If you reject, you may still see
  non-personalised (limited) ads.
- **Some US states** and other regions with privacy laws: you may get Google's notice and the option to opt out of the "sale"
  or "sharing" of your data for targeted advertising.
- **iOS:** the system App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether the game may track you across apps and websites. If you
  choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not available and ads are not personalised with it.
- You can also limit ad tracking in your device settings (Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy &
  Security > Tracking).

**Players under 13**
- Every ad request is tagged as **child-directed** (Google's "tag for child-directed treatment", made for the US Children's
  Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA) and limited to the **G** ad content rating.
- Ads are **non-personalised**: no interest-based advertising and no remarketing.
- No consent form is shown and, on iOS, **no tracking prompt is shown and no IDFA is used**.
- Google limits the data it collects from child-directed requests as described in its policies for families and children.
- Under the GDPR and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, data of children below the age of digital consent in their country
  is not used for personalised advertising.

## 4. Internet time check

To stop the daily limit on bonus-gold videos being reset by changing the phone's clock, the game reads the current time from
the standard `Date` header of a web request to well-known servers (www.google.com, and as fall-backs cloudflare.com and
worldtimeapi.org). Nothing about you is sent in that request beyond what any internet connection reveals (your IP address,
which those servers may log under their own policies). The game does not store or forward the reply apart from the time.

## 5. Motion sensors and vibration

If you turn on MOTION CONTROLS, the game reads your device's accelerometer / gyroscope to steer the plane. Vibration uses the
device's vibration motor. These readings are used on the device in real time only; they are never stored or sent anywhere.

## 6. What I do not do

- No sign-in, no accounts, no cloud saves.
- In-app purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store; the game never sees your payment details.
- No analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs of my own (Unity Analytics and Unity crash reporting are turned off).
- No access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location, and no access to your photo library. A picture you choose for a custom skin comes through your phone's own chooser: the game gets only that picture, keeps a copy on your device and never sends it anywhere.
- I do not sell, rent or trade personal data. Third parties that receive data: Google, through the AdMob SDK, as described above;
  Google Play or the App Store when you buy something; and Unity, when you start a purchase: Unity's purchasing library reports the
  product, the transaction id and your device's model, system version and language to Unity. It does not send your advertising ID.

## 7. Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Children's ads are handled as described in section 3. I do not knowingly collect personal
information from children. If you are a parent and believe your child's data was collected in a way not described here,
contact me at berkcanmakegame@gmail.com and I will help you reach Google, which holds that ad data.

## 8. Security and retention

The game's data stays on the device and is removed when you uninstall. Data collected by Google is sent over encrypted
connections (HTTPS) and kept according to Google's policies.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK in Türkiye, or US state laws), you may have rights to
access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data. As I hold no personal data about you, these requests are
best made to Google for ad data (https://policies.google.com/privacy); I am glad to help - write to berkcanmakegame@gmail.com. You can
change your ad choices at any time in your device's ad settings; on iOS also in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

## 10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

## 11. Contact

Can Make Game - berkcanmakegame@gmail.com

# Winggy Plane - Gizlilik Politikası

politika, oyunun Android ve iOS telefon ve tabletlerde hangi bilgileri işlediğini açıklar. Sorularınız için:
berkcanmakegame@gmail.com.

## Kısaca

- Hesap ve giriş yoktur. Adınızı, e-postanızı, telefon numaranızı, kişilerinizi ya da konumunuzu istemem.
- İlerlemeniz (altınlar, geliştirmeler, uçaklar, ayarlar) **yalnızca cihazınızda** saklanır. Bana hiçbir zaman ulaşmaz.
- Oyun **Google AdMob** reklamları gösterir. Google, reklam göstermek, ölçmek ve dolandırıcılığı önlemek için bazı cihaz ve
  kullanım verilerini toplayabilir - aşağıdaki "Reklamlar" bölümüne bakın.
- 13 yaşından küçük oyunculara yalnızca çocuklara yönelik, kişiselleştirilmemiş reklamlar gösterilir; takip yapılmaz.

## 1. Cihazınızda saklanan bilgiler

Oyun, ilerlemenizi ve tercihlerinizi cihazınızda saklar: altınlar, geliştirmeler, sahip olduğunuz ve seçtiğiniz uçak, en
yüksek skor, ayarlar (dil, karanlık mod, titreşim, solak düzeni, hareket kontrolü hassasiyeti), gördüğünüz eğitim ipuçları,
yaş ekranında girdiğiniz doğum yılı (ya da yalnızca sonucu: 13 yaşın altı veya üstü) ve bugün izlediğiniz bonus altın
videolarının sayısı. Bu veriler uygulamanın kendi özel alanında (bir kısmı şifreli olarak) tutulur ve bana ya da başka
birine gönderilmez.

**Silmek için:** oyunu kaldırın ya da cihaz ayarlarından uygulamanın verilerini temizleyin. Oyunun sakladığı her şey silinir.
Bu verileri hiç almadığım için benim tarafımda silinecek bir şey yoktur.

## 2. Yaş ekranı

Oyunu ilk açtığınızda tarafsız bir ekran doğum yılınızı sorar. Bu bilgi yalnızca cihazınızda, reklamların nasıl
gösterileceğine karar vermek için kullanılır (bölüm 3). Cevabınız bana, Google'a ya da başka birine gönderilmez; reklam
isteklerini yalnızca bunun sonucu ("çocuklara yönelik" olup olmadığı) etkiler.

## 3. Reklamlar (Google AdMob)

Oyun ücretsizdir ve Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited tarafından sağlanan Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) üzerinden
reklam gösterir: ekranın üstünde bir banner, her beşinci FLY AGAIN'den sonra tam ekran bir reklam ve izlemeyi kendiniz
seçtiğiniz ödüllü videolar (TRY AGAIN + SHIELD ve +GOLD).

Reklam gösterebilmek için Google Mobile Ads SDK örneğin şunları toplayabilir ve işleyebilir:
- Android Reklam Kimliği, App Set ID ya da iOS'ta IDFA (yalnızca takibe izin verirseniz) ve IDFV gibi cihaz tanımlayıcıları;
- IP adresiniz; bu adres yaklaşık konumunuzu (ülke / şehir düzeyinde) tahmin etmek için kullanılabilir;
- reklamlarla etkileşiminiz (görüntüleme, dokunma, videonun sonuna kadar izlenmesi);
- SDK ve cihazınızla ilgili tanılama ve performans bilgileri (cihaz modeli, işletim sistemi, uygulama sürümü).

Google bu verileri reklamları sunmak ve ölçmek, ne sıklıkla gösterildiklerini sınırlamak ve dolandırıcılığı ve kötüye
kullanımı tespit etmek için kullanır. Google'ın, hizmetlerini kullanan uygulamalardan gelen bilgileri nasıl kullandığı:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites?hl=tr . Google Gizlilik Politikası:
https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=tr .

**13 yaş ve üstü oyuncular**

- **ABD'nin bazı eyaletleri** ve gizlilik yasası olan diğer bölgeler: Google'ın bildirimini ve verilerinizin hedefli reklam
  için "satışını" veya "paylaşımını" reddetme seçeneğini görebilirsiniz.
- **iOS:** sistemin Uygulama Takip Şeffaflığı (App Tracking Transparency) penceresi, oyunun sizi diğer uygulama ve web

hiçbir şeyi saklamaz ya da iletmez.
